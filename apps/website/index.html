Frequently Asked Questions

Perpetual futures involve leverage and a high risk of loss. Vault returns vary and capital is at risk. All preview data is simulated.

Perpetual futures involve leverage and a high risk of loss. Vault returns vary and capital is at risk. All preview data is simulated.

contact us

Is OpenFutures an exchange?

No. OpenFutures compares venues and routes orders to exchanges.

How does Smart Routing work?

It compares price, fees and depth across six venues for every order, then sends it to the cheapest one.

What can I trade?

Perps across crypto, stocks, indices, commodities and FX.

What is Pro or Prop?

Pro trades your own balance. Prop lets you pass an evaluation to trade capital from the OpenFutures Vault.

How do funded accounts work?

Reach an 8% target within a 5% daily loss limit and 10% max loss over at least 5 trading days. Funded traders keep 80% of profits.

Who funds the vault?

Anyone can deposit USDC and receive vault shares. Returns reflect evaluation fees, profit share and funded losses. Capital is at risk.

Are preview numbers real?

No. OpenFutures is pre-launch. All displayed market, account and vault data is simulated. APY is illustrative, not a promised return.
